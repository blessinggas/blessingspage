Draft a specific application with Blessing (personal statement / cover letter / form answer).

Usage: `/apply <opportunity name>` (e.g. `/apply Martingale Foundation`).

1. Pull that opportunity's details from the register in `index.html` — its
   eligibility (`e`), what the panel wants (`boost`), deadline and link.
2. Pull her positioning from `life-os/dashboard-state.json` (`__pitch`, `__about`)
   and the profile facts (DeepMind Research Ready, EMOS redesign at Edinburgh's
   APRIL AI Hub, fluent French / DELF, first-gen low-income WP status, PrepTrack /
   Harmonia AI builds, beauty-counter commercial instinct).
3. Draft in **her voice** — warm, precise, confident, specific. Map each thing the
   scheme values to concrete evidence from her life. No clichés, no invented facts;
   if something's missing, ask her rather than fabricate.
4. Save the draft to `life-os/applications/<slug>.md` and offer to tighten it,
   change the angle, or cut it to a word limit.

Keep drafts truthful and checkable — these go to real panels. Flag anything that
needs a fact she hasn't given you.
